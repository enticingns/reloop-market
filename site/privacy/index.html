Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details — email address, display name, neighbourhood. Required to post a listing.
  • Listing content — the photos, descriptions and prices you publish, plus the time you published them.
  • Messages — the text of conversations between buyers and sellers on the site.
  • Technical logs — IP address, browser type and requested URL, kept for seven days for abuse and outage investigation.

What we do not do

We do not run third-party advertising or tracking scripts, we do not build profiles of your browsing across other sites, and we do not sell or rent personal data. There are no social login buttons on this site and no embedded video players.

Cookies

Two cookies are set: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a preference cookie that remembers your last search area. Both are first-party and neither is used for advertising. Clearing them signs you out.

How long we keep things

Listings stay up until you remove them or they have been dormant for twelve months. Messages are deleted twelve months after the last reply. Contact form messages are deleted after twelve months. Account records are deleted thirty days after you close your account, except where we need to keep a record of a report about a prohibited item.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of everything we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy@reloop.example from the address on your account and we will answer within thirty days. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the same address handles requests under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

Where the data lives

Account and listing data is stored on a server we rent in Frankfurt, Germany. Backups are encrypted at rest and kept for fourteen days. Email is handled by a separate provider in the European Union.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that affects what we collect, we will email everyone with an account at least fourteen days before the change takes effect.
