How Reloop works

1

Photograph it

Two or three phone photos in daylight are plenty. Buyers trust listings that show the wear honestly.

2

Set your price

Check what similar items went for nearby. Most things move within a week at 40–60% of retail.

3

Hand it over

Meet in a public place, or arrange a doorstep pickup. Cash or instant transfer, whichever you both prefer.
