Common questions

How do I delete a listing? Sign in, open the listing, and choose "Remove". Photos are deleted with it.

Someone did not turn up. Tell us with the listing link. Two no-shows and the account is paused.

Can you hold an item for me? That is between you and the seller — we do not mediate, but we do read reports.

Do you ship? No. Everything on Reloop is collected in person.
