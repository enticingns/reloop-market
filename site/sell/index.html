Photo tips that actually matter

  • Shoot near a window in the morning — no flash, no filters.
  • Clear the background. A tidy frame reads as a cared-for item.
  • Include one photo of the worst mark, close up. Buyers forgive wear; they don't forgive surprises.
  • Put something in the frame for scale if the size isn't obvious.

Pricing without guessing

Open a few similar listings nearby and look at what actually sold, not what is still sitting there. Furniture in good condition usually moves at 40–60% of what you paid; electronics closer to 30–50%, less if the battery has seen better days. Round numbers attract more messages than odd ones, and a price marked "firm" gets fewer low offers than one marked "or nearest offer".

When someone messages

Reply within a day or the listing goes quiet. Agree on a place and time before you share an address, and keep the conversation in Reloop messages until you meet — that way there is a record if something goes wrong.
