About Reloop

Reloop started in 2021 as a shared spreadsheet between four friends in Brooklyn who were all trying to get rid of furniture at the same time. The spreadsheet got too long, someone wrote a proper site, and by the following spring about two thousand people were using it to pass things on.

We are still four people. We do not take a commission, we do not run ads for other companies, and we do not want your item shipped across the country — the whole point is that the buyer is close enough to carry it home.

What we are trying to do

Most second-hand platforms optimise for volume: the more listings, the better, wherever they are. That turns a local marketplace into a mail-order catalogue with worse photos. We go the other way. A listing that cannot be collected on foot or by bus is not much use to anyone, so we keep the map tight and the search local.

Seller guidelines

  • Describe the faults. A scratch you mention is not a problem. A scratch the buyer finds at pickup is an argument.
  • One item per listing. Bundles are fine when they are genuinely a set.
  • No drop-shipping, no new stock. Reloop is for things you already own.
  • No recalled or unsafe goods. Child seats, helmets and anything with a damaged battery are out.
  • Reply to people. Three days of silence and the listing is marked as dormant.

How the site is run

The site is a handful of static pages and a small database, running on one rented server in Frankfurt. That is not a joke about scale — it is genuinely all we need, and it keeps the running cost low enough that nobody has to sell your data to pay for it. Read the privacy page if you want the details.

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, or a listing that should not be there: the contact page reaches all four of us. We answer on weekdays, usually within a day.
