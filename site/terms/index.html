Terms of use

Last updated 4 November 2026.

1. What Reloop is

Reloop is a noticeboard. We let people publish listings for second-hand goods and exchange messages about them. We are not a party to any sale, we do not take payment, and we do not hold money on anyone's behalf. The contract of sale is between the buyer and the seller.

2. Accounts

You need an account to publish a listing or send a message. One account per person. You are responsible for what is posted from your account, and you must be at least 18 years old to use the site.

3. What may not be listed

  • Anything you do not own or are not entitled to sell.
  • New stock, wholesale lots or anything bought specifically to resell.
  • Weapons, medication, tobacco, alcohol, live animals and recalled products.
  • Child seats, helmets and lithium batteries that show damage.
  • Counterfeit goods, or items that infringe someone else's rights.

4. Listings and removals

We may remove a listing, or pause an account, if a listing breaks these terms or if we receive a credible report about it. Where we can, we say why. Repeated breaches end the account.

5. Meetings and risk

Meeting a stranger to exchange goods carries risk, and you take that risk yourself. Meet in public, inspect before paying, and do not send money in advance to someone you have not met. We do not verify the identity of users or the condition of goods.

6. Content you post

You keep the copyright in your photos and text. You give us permission to display them on the site and in search results while the listing is live. That permission ends when you remove the listing.

7. Availability

The site is provided as it is. We aim to keep it up, but we do not promise uninterrupted service, and we are not liable for losses caused by downtime, lost listings or a failed sale.

8. Changes

If these terms change materially we will post the new version here and note the date at the top. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the new terms.
